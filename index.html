ARENA STRIKE

Fight through 15 rooms across three sectors. Clear a room to unlock the door ahead, roll the weapon chest between rooms and pick an upgrade card after each one. Rooms 5, 10 and 15 hold a Warden — destroy the last one to complete the mission.

Left thumb: drag anywhere on the left side to move (push to the edge to sprint).
Right thumb: swipe to look. Hold FIRE and slide to aim while shooting.
AIM toggles sights · JUMP · R reloads · SWAP switches primary/sidearm · KNIFE slashes · USE rolls the weapon chest · Q/E/C grenades · X ultimate · II pauses.
